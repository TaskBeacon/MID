Title: Monetary Incentive Delay (MID) Task
Construct: reward anticipation / feedback processing / motivational control

Purpose:
Show the participant-visible MID trial timeline for win, lose, and neutral incentive conditions.

Rows / conditions:
- Win: magenta reward cue, anticipation fixation, black target, feedback awards +10 on hit or +0 on miss.
- Lose: yellow loss cue, anticipation fixation, black target, feedback gives -0 on hit or -10 on miss.
- Neutral: cyan neutral cue, anticipation fixation, black target, feedback gives +0/-0.

Timeline:
- Cue: 300 ms; no response window; condition-specific colored shape.
- Anticipation: random 1000-1200 ms; early SPACE response is logged and scored as miss; fixation cross.
- Target: adaptive 40-370 ms; SPACE response window; condition-matched black target shape.
- Pre-feedback: random 600-800 ms; no response; fixation cross.
- Feedback: 1000 ms; no response; hit/miss score text based on condition and performance.

Notes:
- Valid key is SPACE.
- Target duration starts at 200 ms and adapts by 30 ms within 40-370 ms toward 66% accuracy, tracked separately by condition.
- Preserve the exact condition labels Win, Lose, Neutral.
